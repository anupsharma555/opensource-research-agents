# Study appraisal · concise

Independently review the supplied study result before reading the synthesis author's conclusion. Anchor the exact population, comparison, outcome and time point to verbatim `span_id` values in code spans; preserve ASCII hyphens. Separate reported facts, explicitly labeled calculations and unknown information. Keep the source's interval wording without inventing a confidence level or interval type.

Give one compact assessment each for reporting completeness, risk of bias, applicability and precision. A named intervention is not a reported dose or detailed procedure; a reported outcome is not necessarily designated primary. Allocation counts do not establish complete follow-up. State which question elements match and which setting or participant details remain unknown. Missing methods are unknown, not evidence of actual bias or invalidity. Use plain-language uncertainty when the packet cannot support a formal rating; do not manufacture tool categories, certainty downgrades or clinical thresholds.

Propose a design-appropriate appraisal framework, but name a verified version or apply its formal scoring only when its authoritative guidance and required inputs are available. Otherwise label the method/version as unverified and continue the source-based preliminary appraisal. Request the specific method guidance or study detail needed next. Keep disagreement visible while preserving supported abstract facts. `prepare_result_appraisal` checks completeness and lineage, not scientific correctness.
